# lonleyfe7randomizer
A randomizer for FE7 with some twists.
Features:

Quality Of Life
- Fallen Units Send Items To Convoy
- Shop In Battle Preps
- Colored Stat Labels
- Items Below Mini Mug

As well as smaller adjustments such as some tutorial scenes skipped. Quality of Life patches from FE Builder such as Global Ranges, Display Growths, All Modes Open, and Disable Tutorials are also pre-installed.

Pre-Configured Randomization For Player And Enemy Units
- Player classes, bases, and growths can be randomized and they will have new palettes suitable for their class.
- Enemy classes can be randomized and will have classes suitable for where they spawn on the map.

Customizable Loot Randomization
- Randomize chests and villages and choose which items will be in each rarity tier.

New Items
- 44 new items, many with special properties or mechanics to shake up the gameplay. Several items are "legendary" items that are limited to one instance per playthrough and have very unique properties such as the blood thirsty Muramasa sword, which allows you to trade your unit's HP for the ability to rewarp! (Okay, so I stole this idea from the Fortune's Weave trailer but it's still cool to see in FE7).

New Houses
- Several houses have updated dialog and a few of them give unique rewards.

Basic Configuration Page
- Configure some parameters of the randomizer such the items that appear in the loot pool, which items are sold at the shop in battle preps and tune the game's difficulty with the chapter difficulty tuner.

The customization options for the randomizer are a little lacking right now, but the intent is not to simply make the game more random but rather to try to make the game more fun to play as a randomizer.

More randomization options will be coming soon, along with more new items, new features, more updated houses and anything else I think of that I feel can improve the experience.

You will need a Windows PC and a clean Fe7 to use the randomizer.
